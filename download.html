T-Deck Plus

LilyGo’s pocket radio, running Ratspeak Handheld.

Firmware

Download.bin Source code↗
  • Messages over LoRa radio or Wi-Fi, with no phone needed.
  • Touchscreen, trackball, and keyboard.
  • Can also be the LoRa radio for Ratspeak on your phone or computer.
Release notes ↗

Before you start

  1. A LilyGo T-Deck PlusDon’t have one? LilyGo store ↗
  2. A USB-C cable that carries dataSome cables only charge, and the computer won’t see the device.
  3. Chrome or Edge on a computerPhones and Safari can’t flash. You can still download the .bin.

Updating Ratspeak Handheld 2.2 or later keeps your messages, settings, and identity. Changing firmware type, or coming from older firmware, needs a fresh install, and the installer asks before it erases anything.
