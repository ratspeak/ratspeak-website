Why Ratspeak

Built without compromise.

Not just another encrypted messenger, but a new protocol for mesh networking. Privacy, identity, and resilient communications are built into the foundation.

No Account

Your identity is a cryptographic key — generated by you, recoverable with your phrase, owned by nobody else. No email, no phone number, no registration.

Privacy First

Messages, packets, routing — encrypted at every layer. Even the network itself can't see what you're sending, or to who.

Transport Agnostic

WiFi, LoRa, 5G, Bluetooth, anything. If it moves data, it works, and they all use the same private transport protocol.

Built Resilient

No servers. No database. No single point of control. The mesh routes around failures on its own.

Multi-Platform

Desktop, mobile, MCU, SBC. One protocol shared across all form factors.

Open Source

Every line of code is public. Read it, review it, fork it. Trust through transparency.
