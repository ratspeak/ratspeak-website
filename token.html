How it started.

We didn’t run a presale or design a token before writing code. It started in the community, and it gave us the freedom to build faster and full-time.

1

Created on Base by the community

In May 2026, a community member deployed RATSPEAK on Base with 100% of supply on the curve and no creator vest. They set the pool up so trading fees could be claimed to support Ratspeak.

Read the original post ↗
2

Support with clear lines

We sat on the fence for a while. Crypto has earned its skepticism. We chose to support the token openly on one condition: the messenger and protocol stay free, open source, and verifiable.

Read our day-one letter →
3

Putting fees to real work

Trading fees went straight into practical work: community node giveaways, donations back to open-source maintainers, devices for testing firmware, and time to keep building.

Token: The truth.

  1. Not a paywall.A paywall.

    Ratspeak stays free. We take communication seriously, so Ratspeak will never be gated behind a token paywall.

  2. Not ownership.Ownership.

    No equity or rights. Holding RATSPEAK grants no ownership, profit share, or legal claim in Ratspeak LLC or its software.

  3. Not a promise.A promise.

    No fake roadmap. We won’t bolt premature token mechanics onto the network just to check boxes or chase hype.

  4. Not a requirement.A requirement.

    Easy to ignore. If you dislike crypto, you can use Ratspeak every day without ever touching a wallet or this page again.

  5. Still building.

    A long-term part of Ratspeak. It already funds open-source work and optional perks. We see room for much larger ideas as more people and nodes come online, and we’ll share them as they develop.

Funding the work

Trading fees go back into building Ratspeak.

  • Node giveaways We send free nodes to people building coverage where they live.
  • Open-source donations We’ve donated more than $1,500 to other open-source projects and communities.
  • Test hardware We buy the boards Ratspeak runs on and test releases on real hardware.
  • Full-time development Fees let us work on Ratspeak full time.

Where it’s going.

Here are some of the ideas we’ve been working on that we can share publicly.

Designing

Readable names

Human-readable names for Ratspeak identities that anyone can verify, even offline.

Designing

Rewarded public nodes

Recognition, and in time rewards, for people who run reliable public relays, propagation nodes, and offline inboxes, based on measured service. Public networks only.

Exploring

Supporter services

Optional extras on our infrastructure, like larger offline inboxes and longer storage. Messaging itself stays free for everyone, always.

Exploring

Ecosystem fund

Funding for developers, node operators, and projects that grow Ratspeak and Reticulum, from apps and integrations to hardware and new coverage.

Exploring

Holder badges in Ratspeak
